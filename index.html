FAQ

Questions, answered.

The things clients usually ask before we start.

Still have a question? Message me. I usually reply quickly. Ask on WhatsApp →
Can you take over my existing Shopify store or website?

Yes. I can fix it, customize it, add features and keep managing it. I start by reviewing what you have so we avoid breaking anything.

Can you build booking or rental sites?

Yes. I add booking buttons, quote and request forms and click-to-call buttons, and set up the pages around them.

Do you use AI? Who checks the work?

I am a modern developer with an assistant and reviewer workflow. AI helps me build, and a set of test and review agents check the work. Then I do my own review before it is finished.

Can I edit the site myself?

On Shopify and WordPress, yes: text, images and prices, without code. On custom React and Next.js sites I make the changes for you.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the size. A fix is quick and a full store takes longer. You get a clear timeline in your quote before we start.

What do I send to start?

Your business name, what you want the site to do, and a site you like. If you only have an idea, send that and we will work out the rest.

What happens after the site is live?

I check that everything works after launch and can keep managing it: updates, new products and fixes.

What hours do you work?

I work US-friendly hours, 9 PM to 6 AM Philippine time. Message me on WhatsApp or email any time and I will reply in those hours.
